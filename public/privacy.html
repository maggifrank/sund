Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Sund is a free app for logging swimming pool visits. It is run by Magnús Franklín, Iceland, who is responsible for your data. Contact: sund@talva.is.

What we collect

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Your email address and nameGoogle or Microsoft, when you sign inTo create your account and let you sign in
Your username and display nameYou choose them; the username starts as your email before the @To address your public page
Your swims: date, time and poolYou, when you log a swimTo count your swims and work out what your cards cost per trip
Your cards: type, price, dates, poolsYou, in settingsThe same
Places you name yourself, with their coordinatesYou, when you name a place that is not on the pool listTo recognise that place next time
Language and display preferencesYouTo show the app the way you set it

We do not collect payment details, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your location

When you log a swim, the app uses your phone's location on the phone itself to find the nearest pool. Only the pool is sent to us, not your position. The one exception is a place you name yourself: you choose to save its coordinates, and they stay private to your account.

Your public page

You choose whether your page at sund.talva.is/username is public, and you can switch it off in settings. When it is on, it shows totals only: how many swims, per month and weekday, per pool, and what your cards work out to per trip. It never shows the time of a swim, which pool you went to on which day, places you named, or your email address.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores the database, in the EU.
  • Netlify serves the website.
  • Google and Microsoft handle sign-in. They tell us your email and name; we never see your password.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank via frankfurter.dev, and no personal data is sent there.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, swims, cards and places immediately; copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct and delete your data from the app's settings at any time. You can also email sund@talva.is for anything the app does not cover. If you think we have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to Persónuvernd, the Icelandic Data Protection Authority, at personuvernd.is.

Browser storage

The app keeps your sign-in session and a copy of your swims in your browser so it works without a connection. Nothing in it is used for tracking. Signing out clears it.

Children

Sund is for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
